A LITTLE SUPPORT, WHENEVER YOU NEED IT

Get the Resettable App

Create checklists, set goals and track your progress at your own pace. Whether you have a few minutes or time for a bigger reset, take it one manageable step at a time.

  • Checklists: break organising into small, practical tasks.
  • Goals: choose what you want to work towards in your space.
  • Timed Reset Sessions: set aside focused time for your next reset.
  • Progress tracking: see the steps you have completed along the way.
Open the App

Free to use directly in your browser. Installation is optional: add it to your Home Screen for easy access.

Your working progress stays in the browser you use. For another browser or device, use the app's backup tools to transfer it.

Open the app first, then follow the installation instructions below. You can also keep using it in your browser without installing it.

Add to your iPhone Home Screen
  1. Open the Resettable app in Safari.
  2. Tap Share (or open the relevant browser menu).
  3. Select Add to Home Screen.
  4. If available, enable Open as Web App, then tap Add.
Install on Android
  1. Open the Resettable app in Chrome.
  2. Open the three-dot menu.
  3. Select Install app or Add to Home screen.
  4. Follow the on-screen instructions.
