FAQS

Good questions to ask before you reset.

What areas do you cover?

Resettable is based in Islington and works across North London and surrounding areas.

Do I need to tidy before you arrive?

No. There is no need to do a pre-reset before your reset. We work with the space as it really is.

Can you help with one cupboard or a whole room?

Yes. A reset can focus on one small area or expand to a larger, more involved space.

Do you only work in homes?

No. Resettable can also help with workspaces, offices, storage areas and shared organisational spaces.

What happens to things I no longer want?

Items can be separated for keeping, donating, recycling or disposing of them according to your wishes.
