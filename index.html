FAQS

Good questions to ask before you reset.

What areas do you cover?

Resettable is focused on Islington and surrounding North London areas.

Do I need to tidy before you arrive?

No. The point is to deal with the space as it really is. There is no need to perform a pre-reset reset.

Can you help with one cupboard or a whole room?

Absolutely. Small, focused resets are a great place to start.

What happens to things I no longer want?

We can separate items for keeping, donating, recycling or disposing of them, according to your wishes.
