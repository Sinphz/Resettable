FAQS

Questions, answered.

What areas do you cover?

Resettable is based in Islington and works across North London. Specific areas can be confirmed when you enquire.

Do I need to tidy before you arrive?

No. There's no need to do a pre-reset before your reset. We'll work with the space as it really is.

Can you help with one cupboard or a whole room?

Absolutely. A reset can focus on one small area or expand to a larger space, depending on what you need.

What happens to things I no longer want?

We'll separate items for keeping, donating, recycling or disposing of them according to your wishes.

How much does a reset cost?

Every reset is different. Tell us about your space and what you'd like help with, and we'll discuss the best option for you.
