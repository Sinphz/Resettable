FAQs

What areas do you cover?

Resettable is based in Islington and works across North London and surrounding areas.

What's the difference between a Quick Reset and a Full Reset?

A Quick Reset is best for smaller, focused jobs. A Full Reset is better for larger or more involved spaces that need deeper sorting and organisation.

How long does a reset take?

It depends on the size and condition of the space. We can discuss this before the reset so you know what to expect.

Do you provide products or storage solutions?

Where useful, practical storage solutions can be discussed as part of the reset.

Is my home or workspace in safe hands?

Yes. Resettable takes a practical, respectful and judgement-free approach, and is DBS checked.
